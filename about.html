The Through-Line

"By deeply understanding the human form through data, we can create physical products that are fundamentally better."

One question, three chapters: How can we use 3D data and advanced manufacturing to move beyond one-size-fits-all products?

That question took me from inventing fully automated custom orthotics at SOLS, to commercializing statistical body models at Body Labs (acquired by Amazon), to revolutionizing how Nike sizes apparel for real human bodies — building a 1.1M body-scan database along the way.

Career Journey

15 years turning body data into products that ship

2013 — 2016

SOLS · Biomechanical Engineer

Co-invented the first fully automated 3D-printed custom orthotic pipeline. Smartphone photo → algorithms → custom CAD → finished product. Scaled to mass manufacturing. Acquired by Aetrex.

2016 — 2017

Body Labs · Product Manager

Commercialized the SMPL statistical body model — state-of-the-art research into a product with eight capabilities across two markets. Built product strategy across evolving input modalities: laser scanners → smartphone cameras → CNN-based inference. Acquired by Amazon.

2017 — 2019

Amazon / AWS · Technical PM

Led migration of Body Labs technology into AWS. Validated ML for Amazon Halo body composition — 2× the accuracy of leading smart scales. Navigated compliance for collecting body photos at scale and facilitated data capture for all Digital Health features.

2019 — 2024

Nike NXT · Digital Product Director

Built a 1.1M body-scan database and question-answering platform that changed how a $50B company sizes its products. Hired Engineering, ML/CV, and Design teams from scratch. Launched the "Fit for Everybody" initiative. Body data became infrastructure at Nike — used from NSRL scientists to marketing directors.

2025 —

Independent Consulting

Helping brands and body-data companies define what digital product creation means for their organization — and building the roadmap to get there. Project-based engagements, fractional director roles, and technology adoption partnerships.
